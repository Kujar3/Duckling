DUCKLING - QUICK START
======================

Duckling is installed. In FL Studio:

1. Options > Manage plugins > "Find installed plugins" (or "Start scan").
2. Open the Mixer (F9), select the track you want to pump (bass, chords,
   pads, a whole bus...) and add Duckling to one of its effect slots.
   Pick the VST3 version.

THE EASY WAY: TEMPO (no routing at all)
- Leave the trigger on TEMPO, pick a shape and a rate (1/4 = every beat,
  like a four-on-the-floor kick) and press play. That's it.
- Depth sets how far the volume dips. 100 % = all the way to silence.
- "Duck: Lows" ducks only the low end (below the split), so the bass gets
  out of the kick's way while the top of the sound stays steady.

FOLLOW YOUR REAL KICK: AUDIO
1. Mixer: select your kick's track, right-click the small arrow under the
   track that has Duckling and choose "Sidechain to this track".
2. Duckling's window: click the gear icon (wrapper settings) > Processing
   tab, and pick the kick's track as the sidechain input.
3. Play. The panel in the bottom right says "Listening" when the kick
   arrives. Drag the threshold line just under the kick's peaks.

DRAW YOUR OWN PATTERN: MIDI
1. Channel rack: (+) > MIDI Out, and set its Port (for example 1).
2. Duckling's window: gear icon (wrapper settings) > Settings tab, and set
   the MIDI Input port to the same number.
3. Draw notes in the MIDI Out channel's pattern. Every note is one duck;
   the pitch doesn't matter.

In Audio and MIDI mode the rate chooser becomes LENGTH: how long each duck
lasts before the volume is fully back.

The "?" button in the top-right corner of the plugin shows these steps too.
Scroll over the big curve to browse the shapes.
